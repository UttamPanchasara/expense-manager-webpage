Privacy Policy

Expense Manager

Expense Manager ("App", "we", "our", or "us") is a personal finance application designed to help users manage expenses, income, budgets, savings goals, recurring transactions, payment methods, and financial records.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is stored, processed, and shared when you use Expense Manager on Android or iOS.

Expense Manager is designed with privacy and local data storage in mind. We do not operate or maintain our own backend servers or databases for storing users' financial records.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

1Information We Collect

Expense Manager primarily stores your financial information locally on your device.

We do not directly collect or centrally store your personal financial records on developer-operated servers.

Depending on the features you use and the third-party services integrated into the App, certain technical, diagnostic, advertising, or service-related information may be processed by third-party providers.

1.1 Financial Information

You may enter information such as:

  • Expenses and income
  • Budgets and savings goals
  • Categories and payment methods
  • Transaction descriptions and notes
  • Recurring transactions and reminders
  • Receipt images and attachments
  • Financial information associated with different profiles
  • Account and payment-related information

This information is stored locally on your device in the App's database.

Your financial records are not automatically transmitted to any developer-operated server.

1.2 Technical and Diagnostic Information

Depending on the third-party services enabled in the App, certain technical information may be processed, including:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version and technical identifiers
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information
  • Advertising identifiers, where available and permitted
  • IP address and related network information
  • Advertising impressions, clicks, and interactions
  • Information required for service functionality and fraud prevention

The information processed depends on the third-party SDKs, their configuration, and your device privacy settings.

2Information You Provide

Information you manually enter into Expense Manager is used to provide the App's functionality.

Your financial records are stored locally on your device.

The App supports multiple profiles, allowing you to organize and manage financial records separately.

Expense Manager does not operate a developer-managed backend server. Information entered into the App remains on your device unless you explicitly use a third-party service, such as Google Drive Backup & Restore, to transfer or store backup data.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate backups of important financial information.

3How We Use Your Information

Information stored within the App is used to provide its features and functionality, including:

  • Managing expenses and income
  • Creating and managing budgets
  • Tracking savings goals
  • Managing recurring transactions
  • Generating financial reports and statements
  • Managing payment methods
  • Attaching receipts and supporting documents
  • Scheduling reminders and notifications
  • Providing backup and restore functionality
  • Protecting access to the App
  • Verifying premium purchase entitlements

Third-party services may process technical, diagnostic, advertising, or purchase-related information to provide their respective services.

Your locally stored financial records are not used by us for advertising or marketing.

4Data Storage and Security

Expense Manager stores financial information locally on your device.

The App uses local database storage and applicable security mechanisms to help protect stored information.

Depending on the platform and device, security features may include:

  • Local database encryption
  • Device-level security
  • Biometric authentication using Face ID or Touch ID on supported iOS devices
  • Fingerprint authentication on supported Android devices

Expense Manager does not synchronize your financial records with any developer-operated server.

Your data remains within the local application storage unless you choose to export, share, or back it up using an available third-party service.

We do not receive or store your fingerprint or facial biometric templates. Biometric authentication is handled through the device's operating system.

Although we take reasonable steps to protect locally stored information, no storage or security mechanism can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate device security and keeping backups of important financial records.

5Permissions

The App requests permissions only when required for a particular feature.

Available permissions and functionality may differ between Android and iOS.

5.1 Camera and Photos

Camera or photo library access may be requested to capture or select receipt images and attach them to transactions.

Access is requested when you use the relevant functionality.

5.2 Notifications

Notification permissions are used for reminders, scheduled expenses, recurring transactions, and other user-configured alerts.

5.3 Biometric Authentication

The App may use the device's supported biometric authentication mechanism to protect access to the application.

On iOS, this may include Face ID or Touch ID. On Android, this may include fingerprint or other supported biometric authentication.

Biometric verification is handled by the operating system.

5.4 Google Sign-In and Google Drive

Google authentication is used when you choose to connect your Google Account for optional backup and restore functionality.

The App accesses only the permissions required for the selected functionality.

6Advertising

Expense Manager may display advertisements to users who have not purchased the Premium Lifetime upgrade.

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob and its associated technologies may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers, where available and permitted
  • Device and application information
  • IP address and related network information
  • Advertising impressions, clicks, and interactions
  • Information used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and diagnostics

The precise information processed depends on Google's services, advertising configuration, applicable consent requirements, and your device privacy settings.

On iOS, advertising tracking is subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency requirements where applicable.

If the App performs tracking that requires permission under Apple's rules, it will request the appropriate permission before performing such tracking.

You can manage applicable advertising and privacy settings through your device settings.

Users who purchase Premium Lifetime can remove advertisements from the App.

For more information about Google's data practices, please refer to:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

  • App version

  • Diagnostic events
  • Technical information required to investigate application issues

These services help identify technical problems, monitor application stability, and improve reliability.

  • Enable premium features

9Google Drive Backup and Restore

Google Drive Backup & Restore is an optional feature.

If you choose to connect your Google Account and use this functionality:

  • The App requests the necessary Google authentication and Drive permissions.
  • Backup files are uploaded to the designated Expense Manager backup location in your Google Drive.
  • Backup files may contain financial records, profile information, and associated attachments.
  • You can restore your backup when needed.
  • Backup operations communicate directly with Google's services.

Expense Manager does not operate an intermediary backup server or maintain a separate central database of your backup files.

Your use of Google Drive is also subject to Google's terms and privacy practices.

You can disconnect Expense Manager from your Google Account through your Google Account security settings.

You can manage or delete Expense Manager backup data through Google Drive and the available backup management functionality.

To manage third-party application access, visit:

https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

10Data Retention and Deletion

Your financial records are stored locally on your device and remain there until you delete them or remove the App and its associated local application data.

Expense Manager does not maintain a central developer-operated database of users' financial records.

10.1 Deleting Local Information

You can remove locally stored information by:

  • Using the relevant data deletion or reset functionality provided within the App, where available.
  • Uninstalling the App to remove its locally stored application data.

Please export or back up important information before deleting local data.

10.2 Deleting Google Drive Backups

If you have used Google Drive Backup & Restore, backup files may remain in your Google Drive account even after uninstalling Expense Manager.

You can delete these backup files directly from Google Drive or manage the application's access through your Google Account settings.

10.3 Disconnecting Google Account Access

To disconnect Expense Manager from your Google Account, visit:

https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Locate Expense Manager and remove its access if you no longer wish to use Google Drive integration.

10.4 Privacy-Related Requests

For questions or assistance concerning your information and privacy, you can contact us at:

vappsolution@gmail.com

Please note that deleting the App does not automatically delete backup files stored in Google Drive or purchase records retained by Apple or Google.
